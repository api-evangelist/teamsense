# TeamSense

> TeamSense is a text-first, fully automated absence management and attendance tracking platform built for frontline workers. Employees report absences via SMS — no app, no login, no training required. The system automatically notifies supervisors, triggers workflows, and integrates with existing HR and payroll systems.

## Core Product

- [Absence Management](https://www.teamsense.com/absence-management/): Automate absence reporting and notifications via text message for frontline workers
- [Attendance Tracking](https://www.teamsense.com/attendance-tracking/): Real-time attendance visibility for supervisors and HR teams
- [Compliance & Documentation](https://www.teamsense.com/compliance/): Automated call-off documentation and policy enforcement
- [Integrations](https://www.teamsense.com/integrations/): Connect with ADP, SAP, Kronos, UKG, and other HR/payroll systems
